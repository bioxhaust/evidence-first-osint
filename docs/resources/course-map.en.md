# Course map

[Русский](course-map.md) · [English overview](../en/index.md)

**From a question to a reproducible report.** There are 18 complete modules: 17 required and one optional Python module. **Lessons, labs and solutions are in Russian.** This map and the core reference materials are available in English; the full course is not translated.

Required lessons, including their labs: **17 h 15 min**. Three 90-minute projects add 4 h 30 min; the capstone adds 4 hours. Planned minimum: **25 h 45 min**. Allow **26–34 hours** including revisions. Optional Python adds 75 minutes. These are editorial estimates, not measured completion data.

## 1. Design the investigation

- **[01. Question and hypotheses](../lessons/01-question.md)** · 60 min, Russian. A testable question, scope and competing hypotheses.

- **[02. Ethics and safety](../lessons/02-ethics.md)** · 60 min, Russian. A permitted scope, data minimization and stopping rules.

- **[03. Search and archives](../lessons/03-search.md)** · 60 min, Russian. Multilingual queries and a comparison of archived versions.

- **[04. Sources and cognitive bias](../lessons/04-sources.md)** · 60 min, Russian. A provenance map and an independence check.

- **[05. Preserving evidence](../lessons/05-evidence.md)** · 60 min, Russian. Originals, working copies, a journal and an integrity check.


**Checkpoint:** [project 01](../practice/projects/01.md), 90 min, Russian. Attempt it before reading the solution.

## 2. Verify visual evidence

- **[06. Images](../lessons/06-images.md)** · 60 min, Russian. Caption, derivative-image and metadata checks.

- **[07. Video](../lessons/07-video.md)** · 60 min, Russian. A frame index and bounded findings from footage.

- **[08. Geolocation](../lessons/08-geolocation.md)** · 60 min, Russian. A comparison of candidate locations using landmark relationships.

- **[09. Chronolocation](../lessons/09-chronolocation.md)** · 60 min, Russian. A timeline separating publication, capture and event times.

- **[10. Maps and satellite imagery](../lessons/10-maps.md)** · 60 min, Russian. An explanation of scale, image date and precision limits.


**Checkpoint:** [project 02](../practice/projects/02.md), 90 min, Russian. Attempt it before reading the solution.

## 3. Connect records and relationships

- **[11. Companies and procurement](../lessons/11-companies.md)** · 60 min, Russian. Registry, contract and payment joins using stable IDs.

- **[12. Domains, DNS and certificates](../lessons/12-infrastructure.md)** · 60 min, Russian. A passive infrastructure record without invented attribution.

- **[13. Public platform content](../lessons/13-social.md)** · 60 min, Russian. Public-post analysis with sampling and repost limitations.

- **[14. Entities and relationships](../lessons/14-entities.md)** · 60 min, Russian. Typed relationships and rejected false matches.

- **[15. Data cleaning](../lessons/15-data.md)** · 60 min, Russian. A reproducible calculation with exclusions and quarantine.


**Checkpoint:** [project 03](../practice/projects/03.md), 90 min, Russian. Attempt it before reading the solution.

## 4. Check and communicate findings

- **[16. Python and APIs — optional](../lessons/16-python.md)** · 75 min, Russian. The same calculation in code; a permitted API with a local fallback.

- **[17. Disinformation and AI](../lessons/17-disinformation-ai.md)** · 75 min, Russian. A check of compound claims and fabricated AI evidence.

- **[18. Reporting and confidence](../lessons/18-reporting.md)** · 60 min, Russian. A report tracing each finding to evidence and limitations.

## Capstone

[North Crossing](../practice/capstone.md), Russian: assess four claims from one post, cite at least eight evidence items, examine two alternatives and write a 900–1400-word report. All entities are fictional. Unknown ownership and exact image time can legitimately remain unknown.
