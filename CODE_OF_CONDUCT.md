# Community code of conduct

We welcome people with different backgrounds, experience and languages. Critique evidence and ideas, not a person's identity or worth. Explain corrections constructively and respect a contributor's decision not to share personal details.

Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, publish private information, share leaked credentials, target private individuals or post unsolicited promotions. Do not upload graphic or sensitive material as a demonstration; describe the issue with a safe synthetic reproduction.

For ordinary moderation concerns, use GitHub's reporting controls or a minimal issue that contains no identifying details about affected people. For sensitive cases, use the repository's private vulnerability reporting channel if available; do not disclose personal information publicly. The project does not promise an emergency response or confidentiality beyond the capabilities of the channel used.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, warn participants, restrict participation or block accounts. Actions should be proportionate to the behavior and protect affected people. Good-faith disagreement and corrections are welcome. GitHub's own community guidelines also apply.

These rules apply in this repository's issues, pull requests and other spaces explicitly maintained for this project.
